Show formulas and emission-factor assumptions

Passenger-kilometre emissions = (vehicle tailpipe factor in g CO₂ / vehicle-km ÷ assumed occupancy) × passenger-km. Avoided kg = (baseline g − alternative g) ÷ 1,000. Annual estimate = monthly estimate × 12.

Factors used: two-wheeler 29 g/km; three-wheeler 78 g/km; private four-wheeler 140 g/km; bus 788 g/km. Bus and shared-vehicle estimates allocate vehicle emissions across assumed occupants. These are indicative tailpipe CO₂ factors from the India EV Digest 2023 document citing CPCB/MoHUA material; actual fleet, fuel, load and driving conditions vary. Negative savings mean the selected alternative has a higher estimated per-passenger tailpipe factor.

Walking/cycling are treated as zero direct tailpipe emissions for the trip, not zero lifecycle emissions.

06 / INDOOR AIR HUB

Make the air at home work harder for you.

Indoor exposure depends on outdoor infiltration and pollution created inside. These practical steps focus on source control first.

⌁

Ventilate at the right time

When outdoor air is cleaner, open windows for a practical period. During high outdoor pollution, reduce unnecessary outdoor-air entry — but do not compromise safe ventilation, combustion safety or building requirements.

Timing matters
◌

Filter particles safely

Choose a mechanical HEPA cleaner sized for the room, keep doors/windows managed, and maintain filters. Avoid ionisers or ozone-generating devices marketed as air cleaners.

Mechanical filtration
✧

Control indoor sources

Use an externally vented kitchen exhaust where possible. Avoid indoor smoking, incense and candles. Keep cooking emissions down and never block required combustion-air or ventilation openings.

Source reduction first
≋

Clean and manage dampness

Wet-mop and damp-wipe to limit dust resuspension. Fix leaks, dry damp areas and keep humidity comfortable. Avoid mixing cleaning chemicals; follow product labels.

Simple routines
!

Do not seal a home unsafely. Ventilation needs depend on the building, fuel appliances and outdoor conditions. If you have fuel-burning appliances, maintain required ventilation and carbon-monoxide safety. Seek qualified advice for persistent dampness, mould or ventilation problems.

08 / ABOUT & METHODOLOGY

Trust is part of clean air.

ClearSkies separates measured facts, model outputs, assumptions and AI-generated suggestions.

Air-quality data

Open-Meteo Air Quality API provides CAMS model pollutant forecasts. Values are modelled estimates, not direct CPCB station readings. The app calculates an indicative sub-index using CPCB concentration breakpoints from available trailing hourly model values, and labels the result accordingly. It does not represent an official regulatory AQI.

Open-Meteo API documentation ↗

Indian AQI categories

India's National AQI categories are Good (0–50), Satisfactory (51–100), Moderate (101–200), Poor (201–300), Very Poor (301–400) and Severe (401–500). The CPCB method uses pollutant-specific sub-indices; the maximum valid sub-index determines the AQI.

CPCB AQI methodology ↗

Emissions methodology

Transport calculator uses documented tailpipe CO₂ factors as a transparent approximation. Passenger occupancy is adjustable. Monthly rupee savings are calculated only from the household's own entered cost estimates. It does not calculate total lifecycle CO₂e, local pollutant exposure or citywide AQI change.

India EV Digest 2023 source ↗

AI limitations & privacy

Gemini is optional and called only by the server. The app sends your question and the saved household profile when you submit a question. Do not include sensitive health details. If Gemini is unavailable, deterministic local guidance remains available. AI suggestions are not medical advice.
